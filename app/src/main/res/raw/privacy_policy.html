Focus – Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Focus ("the app") is a doomscrolling-prevention app that helps you block distracting apps and reclaim your attention. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

Data We Collect

Focus stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Whether Focus Mode is currently active
  • Which apps you have chosen to block (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok)
  • Your chosen blocking mode per app (Off, Block, Friends Only)
  • Temporary unlock timestamps
  • Whether you have an active premium subscription (cached locally)

None of this data is transmitted off your device or shared with any third party.

Usage Access Permission

Focus requests Android's Usage Access permission (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS). This permission lets the app detect which app is currently in the foreground so it can show a blocking screen when you open a blocked app. This information is processed entirely on your device and is never stored or transmitted.

Display Over Other Apps Permission

Focus requests the Appear on Top permission (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW). This allows the blocking overlay to appear over other apps. It is used solely to display the gate screen when a blocked app is opened during Focus Mode.

Google Play Billing

If you subscribe to Focus Premium, the subscription is managed by Google Play. Google's own privacy policy applies to that transaction. Focus receives only a confirmation of whether a valid subscription exists — no payment details are ever seen or stored by the app.

No Analytics, No Ads

Focus does not include any analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools. We do not collect usage statistics, crash reports, or any behavioural data.

Data Deletion

All app data is stored in Android's private app storage. Uninstalling the app deletes all data permanently.
